PoetBoard

A modern, open-source keyboard for Android — with predictive typing, a huge emoji picker, six soft pastel themes and a one-tap AI fix button.

Overview

PoetBoard is an Android Input Method Editor (IME) built on a custom View-based architecture (no deprecated KeyboardView). It ships with a 250,000-word AOSP English dictionary, learns as you type, and keeps everything private and on-device.

AI fix (WordWise)

The ✦fix button in the suggestion bar types the ?fix trigger — a shortcut for the WordWise AI-powered, system-wide grammar assistant, which rewrites the text you just typed. PoetBoard itself sends nothing anywhere; it only types the trigger.

How predictive text works

  • You type on the T9 keys.
  • Candidates are gathered from your learned dictionary, the AOSP dictionary, and (optionally) your contacts.
  • They are ranked by AOSP freq + learned freq × recency + bigram boost.
  • The best word is shown inline — tap space or a suggestion to commit and it's learned.

Features

  • Predictive text & auto-capitalization
  • Symbols panel, categorized emoji picker with search, and a text-editing panel
  • Six pastel themes — Soft Peach, Blush Rose, Lavender Mist, Sky Blue, Mint & Sky, Butter Cream
  • Adjustable haptics, key sounds, delete speed and font sizes
  • AI fix shortcut for the WordWise grammar assistant

Security & Privacy

AreaProtection
Key at restlearned_words & t9_prefs encrypted with SafeBox (ChaCha20-Poly1305), hardware-backed keystore
BackupsEncrypted stores excluded from cloud backup & device transfer
Sensitive fieldsPassword, numeric, phone & hinted fields skip learning and suggestions
Crash logsSanitized, no typed content, capped at 200 lines
RetentionLearned words hard-expire after 180 days

You can also blacklist specific apps under Settings → Privacy so words typed there are never learned.
