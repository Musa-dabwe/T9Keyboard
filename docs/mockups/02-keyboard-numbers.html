No collision here

Number mode itself is fine

Unlike letter mode, SPACE's primary label never gets overwritten by numLabel — KeyLabelRenderer only rewrites the 14 letter/punctuation keys, so SPACE stays "SPACE" here and there's no visible duplicate "0". The collision in the review only happens in letter mode, where both QR's and SPACE's corner hints show "0" at the same time.

Worth knowing

Two symbols aren't real digits

26 letters over 13 pairs plus punctuation need 14 single-character codes, but a phone dial pad only has 10 digits. WX and YZ had to borrow , and . instead of a digit — reasonable here since they're genuinely useful punctuation, but it's why this grid can never be a literal 0–9 dial pad.
