Bug

QR / SPACE both show "0"

As shipped: both keys display 0 simultaneously — visible in the screenshot. Proposed: QR keeps 0 (it's the real numeric-page digit); SPACE's hint switches to . and its long-press now inserts a period instead of a redundant digit.

Accessibility

Adaptive on-accent content color

Cycle the accent swatches above. As shipped: Backspace/Shift content is hard-coded white — try yellow or teal and watch it nearly disappear. Proposed: content color is picked from the accent's relative luminance (WCAG), flipping to near-black when the accent is light.

Hierarchy

Enter's emoji hint toned down

As shipped: the 🙂 hint renders at full emoji weight, heavier than every digit hint on the board. Proposed: shrunk and desaturated to sit at the same visual level as 1–9.

Spec drift

Toolbar: muted until active

As shipped: Settings and Edit icons are always accent-tinted (SuggestionBar.setAccentColor() tints both unconditionally). Proposed: both default to the muted hint color; Edit switches to accent only while the editing panel is open. The xt9 chip becomes a pill outline so it reads as a toggle even when off.
